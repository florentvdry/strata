# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Strata to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- Being kind, patient and respectful, including when you disagree.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Focusing on what's best for the people who use and build Strata.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer through a [private security advisory](https://github.com/florentvdry/strata/security/advisories/new) (mark it as a conduct report), or by contacting [@florentvdry](https://github.com/florentvdry) on GitHub. All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this Code of Conduct. Depending on severity, they may give a warning, or temporarily or permanently ban someone from the project.
